# House Style

Notes are built in Word and published as PDF. The layout follows professional research formatting, modelled on CFA Institute Research Challenge winning reports and broker initiations. Adopted in November 2026; the TYL initiation predates the exhibit-rail grid.

## Page

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Paper | A4 portrait, 2.0 cm side margins |
| Grid | 10.2 cm text column, 0.5 cm gutter, 6.3 cm exhibit rail |
| Rule | Each exhibit sits in the rail beside the paragraph that cites it. Full width only for wide tables, the financial summary and grids |
| Cover | Always one page: rating box, key data, 12-month price chart against the index, five-year key financials |

## Type and colour

| Element | Setting |
|---|---|
| Headings | Helvetica, navy |
| Body | Arial 9.5pt, left-aligned, never justified |
| Exhibits | Arial 7.5 to 8pt |
| Palette | Navy `#0B2545`, steel `#2F5F8F`, sky `#8DB3DB`, peer grey `#A6ACB3` |
| Negatives | Burgundy `#8B1A1A`, and burgundy is used for nothing else |
| Never | Orange, the covered company's brand colours, pie charts, 3D, icons, photos |

## Exhibits

- **Every exhibit has the same furniture,** in this order: a numbered title that states a conclusion ("Exhibit 4.2: Power, not land, decides who builds"), a unit line, the chart or table, then a source line.
- **Emphasis:** the subject company in navy, peers in grey.
- **Labels are section-based** (Exhibit 8.2), so exhibits can be added later without renumbering the whole note.
- **Forecast columns are tinted.** Years carry A or E (FY26A, FY27E).

## Voice

1. **Titles state conclusions,** not topics.
2. **Each paragraph opens with a bold lead sentence.** A reader who skims only the leads still gets the argument.
3. **A number behind every adjective.** Not "strong growth" but "+11.2%".
4. **Australian English, editorial "we",** on US names too.
5. **About one exhibit per page.** Tables carry the argument; prose interprets it.
