**How this is measured**

- **Moves are absolute share price changes** from the note's reference price, excluding dividends, and are not adjusted for the benchmark.
- **Falsifier status** is read from the company's filings each quarter.
  - *Intact:* nothing disclosed points towards the condition.
  - *At risk:* a disclosed figure has moved towards it.
  - *Breached:* the condition is met on its own terms.
- **A breached falsifier is final.** It is not reinterpreted after the event.
- **One record, good or bad.** Calls that go wrong stay in the table with the ones that go right.
