# Initiation Structure

An initiation runs to about 24 pages of content in one PDF, plus disclosures. The structure was built by tearing down published broker initiations and results notes, keeping what they do well and closing the gaps they leave: no stated falsifiers, scenario boxes without probabilities, and sensitivity grids that contradict the conclusion beside them.

| 3 | Thesis in pictures | 1.00 | Four exhibits whose captions read as one argument |
| 4 | Industry and market structure | 2.00 | How the industry earns, what is changing, market size, and the bear case on the industry |
| 5 | The business | 1.00 | Segment mix, the economic chain from input to cash, customer concentration |

About 31% of the content sits in the pivotal questions, up from 20% in the first version of the structure.

This is version 2, adopted in November 2026. The TYL initiation was published on version 1 (16 sections, 18 pages); the next initiation is the first on version 2.

## Pivotal questions

Each initiation turns on three questions. A question qualifies only if it passes all four tests:

| Test | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Closed | Answerable yes or no |
| Thresholded | Contains a number |
| Dated | Contains a horizon |
| Rating-relevant | A wrong answer changes the rating |

"How will AI affect its competitive position?" fails the first three. "Will organic SaaS growth hold at or above 20% in each of FY2026 to FY2028?" passes all four.

## The four gates

Drafting stops at four points until the analyst's judgement goes in. A gate is never passed on an assumption.

| Gate | After | Requires | Unblocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | Context sections | The three pivotal questions and a provisional answer to each | The pivotal question sections |
| G2 | What's priced in | The variant view, written off the reverse DCF and the gap to consensus | Valuation and scenarios |
| G3 | Scenarios | Rating, conviction, scenario probabilities, the three falsifiers | Risks, Red Team, conclusion, cover |
| G4 | Disclosures | A full tie-out: 27 checks, see [quality control](quality-control.md) | Publication |

## Ratings
