# Investment Policy (Summary)

The research is paired with an **AUD 10,000 notional portfolio**, governed by a written investment policy statement adopted before inception on 1 September 2026. The portfolio exists to make the research accountable: each call either becomes a position under fixed rules or it doesn't, and the outcome is measured.

## Objective and benchmark

| Item | Policy |
|---|---|
| Return objective | Benchmark plus 300 basis points a year, time-weighted, in AUD, gross |
| Benchmark | 50% S&P 500 Total Return (in AUD, unhedged), 50% S&P/ASX 200 Accumulation, rebalanced quarterly |
| Why total return | The gap between price and accumulation indices (about 1.5% a year in the US, 4% in Australia) is larger than the whole objective |
| Evaluation horizon | Three years minimum |
| Risk control | Structural concentration limits, not volatility targets |

## Eligible universe

US and ASX primary listings, market cap of about US$2bn or more, technology and tech-adjacent preferred. Banks, insurers and REITs are excluded. A name below the floor can be covered as research but never held.

## Position sizing

| Conviction tier | Target weight | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 5% | Credible thesis, but a falsifier is near-term and unresolved |
| Core | 10% | The standard Buy: thesis complete, falsifiers testable but not imminent |
| High conviction | 15% | Specific variant view, wide reverse DCF divergence, well-understood downside |

The tier is a testable prediction. If high-conviction positions don't beat starter positions over time, that is a finding about the process.

## Entry and exit

**Entry.** A position opens only on a published Buy (expected 12-month return above +12%). The test is applied at the publication-date price, not the note's reference price. A decision journal entry is written before the trade.

**Mandatory exit.** Any one of:

- a falsifier is breached (one is enough, and it can't be reinterpreted after the fact)
- the rating is cut to Sell
- the target price is reached and no revised target is published within ten business days

**Price-only selling is prohibited.** A fall in the share price is a reason to review, never on its own a reason to sell.

**Post-mortem.** Every exit gets a written post-mortem within a week.

## Limits

| Limit | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Single position at cost | 15% |
| Single position at market | 20%, trim to 15% |
| Single GICS sector at cost | 60% |
| Single GICS sector at market | 75%, trim to 60% |
| Number of holdings | 8 to 12 |
| Portfolio drawdown | 30% triggers a written review |

## Currency

Every non-AUD position reports its local return, its currency contribution and its total AUD return separately. At a 3% alpha target, currency moves would otherwise be indistinguishable from stock selection.

## Status

In cash since inception. The first published Buy (TYL) did not clear the +12% hurdle at its publication-day price, so it did not enter.
